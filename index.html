About the Founders

Thomas E. Walker, MDiv. & Denise Ford-Walker

“

Reflections of Grace Outreach Ministries exists to be a mirror of God's grace in our community — a 501c3 nonprofit that believes in helping as a servant of God, meeting people where they are, lifting up the discouraged, and walking alongside families in every season of life. We are Ministers (Servants) that encourages others to have faith, believe in the Goodness of God, and believe that all things are possible.

Thomas E. Walker, MDiv. — Founder

Denise Ford-Walker — Co-Founder

Pastor · Author · Educator · Co-Founders

Thomas E. Walker holds a Master of Divinity in Pastoral Counseling and serves as the founder of Reflections of Grace Outreach Ministries, Inc., a 501c3 nonprofit based in Moreno Valley, California.

He is the author of Generation X Left the Church, Now All Hell Broke Loose (2025, ISBN: 978-0-9830162-8-1) — available as an audiobook on ElevenReader.

Co-Founder Denise Ford-Walker is a minister, songwriter, and author producing Inspirational Melodies in Song, Books, and Audiobooks. Google her name and you will be blessed.
