About This Ministry

"The Word of God is living and powerful, sharper than any two-edged sword." — Hebrews 4:12

Reflections of Grace Outreach Ministries, Inc. is a 501(c)3 nonprofit ministry founded by Thomas E. Walker, MDiv. and co-founder Minister Denise Ford-Walker, based in Moreno Valley, California.

Our mission is to make ministry-grade biblical education accessible to every believer, everywhere, at absolutely no cost. The Training Portal represents years of curriculum development rooted in Scripture, theology, counseling, and lived ministry experience.

Thomas E. Walker, MDiv.

Thomas holds a Master of Divinity and brings decades of pastoral experience to this curriculum. His teaching covers biblical foundations, the vacuum effect, counseling integration, evangelism, and servant leadership.

Minister Denise Ford-Walker

Denise co-founded Reflections of Grace and authored the prayer curriculum in Phase 07 — Praying to Know Him, Praying From My Soul — an eight-lesson journey into the depths of communion with God.

Our Commitment

Every lesson, quiz, resource, and tool on this portal is free. No subscription. No tuition. No payment ever required. This is ministry without walls, and the door is always open.
